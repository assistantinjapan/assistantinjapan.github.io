Questions

Before you send a request

Can I call from a foreign SIM?

Yes. The service works from any phone that can make calls in Japan, including a foreign SIM with roaming. Your own carrier may still charge you for roaming.

How does a 3-way call work?

You send a request. We arrange the call, and our interpreter joins the call with you and the other party.

When do I pay?

Payment details are confirmed with you before the call.

How we work

Clear words, and care for the details

Our professional interpreters listen carefully. We pass on both the meaning and the intent. We do not add, leave out, or soften what was said. We stay discreet, and we keep the focus on your conversation.

We also know the kinds of calls people face in Japan: a clinic visit, a form at the ward office, a call with a company or a public office. That helps the conversation go more smoothly.
