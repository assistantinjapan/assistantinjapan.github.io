About Assistant in Japan

An over-the-phone interpreting service for people and organizations who need to communicate clearly in Japan — in Japanese, English, and Spanish.

The interpreting is provided by a long-time resident of Japan, with JLPT N1 certification and business-level Japanese, who also works in English and Spanish. Living here for many years means more than fluency: it means knowing the pace of a consultation at a clinic, the forms that come up at a ward office, and the tone that is expected on a call with a company or a public institution.

Interpreting is not word-for-word substitution. It is listening carefully, choosing the right register, and passing on both the meaning and the intent — without adding, leaving out, or softening what was said. We stay discreet, keep the focus on your conversation, and aim for accuracy you can rely on.

Who it is for

  • Residents who are comfortable in daily life but want support for official, medical, or technical calls
  • Visitors or newcomers facing a situation they did not expect
  • Companies and professionals who need to speak with Japanese counterparts by phone
  • Spanish-speaking and English-speaking clients alike, whenever Japanese is on the other end of the line — or the other way around

A note on the work

We interpret over the phone. We are not a law firm, a medical practice, or an immigration consultancy, and we do not give legal, medical, or financial advice. We make sure the conversation itself is understood.

If you have a call coming up, or one happening now, we are here to help.
