Services

Services and pricing

Assistant in Japan provides over-the-phone interpreting in two language pairs: Japanese and English, and Japanese and Spanish. You can call when you need us, or schedule a session in advance.

On-demand phone interpreting

For situations that come up without warning — a clinic calling back, a delivery, a problem with a bill, or a conversation you did not expect to need help with. Call +81 90-6534-8689. We will confirm the language pair and join the call as quickly as we can.

If we are already on another call, we will get back to you as soon as we are free. For anything time-sensitive, calling is the fastest way to reach us.

Scheduled phone interpreting

For appointments you can plan: a hospital visit, a meeting at city hall, a call with a landlord, a bank appointment, or a business meeting. Tell us the date, time (including the time zone, if you are calling from abroad), language pair, and what the call is about. We will confirm and be ready at the agreed time.

How a call works

Most clients use a three-way call: you, the other party, and the interpreter on the same line. We can also place the call with you, or you can call us first and we will add the other party. If you are unsure which option fits, we will help you set it up.

What we interpret

Typical requests include hospitals and clinics, city hall and immigration paperwork, conversations with landlords and real-estate agents, banks and phone carriers, business calls, and schools. If your situation is a little different, write or call and we can tell you whether we can help.

Language pairs

We interpret Japanese ⇔ English and Japanese ⇔ Spanish, always in both directions. We do not offer English–Spanish interpreting without Japanese.

Pricing

Rates depend on the language pair, whether the session is on-demand or scheduled, the length of the call, and the type of appointment. We do not publish a single fixed price, because the work is not always the same.

Contact us for a quote. For a scheduled session, include the date, expected length, and a brief description of the situation, and we will get back to you with a clear figure. For on-demand calls, we can explain the rate when you phone us, before interpreting begins.
