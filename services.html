When you need us now

For the calls that appear without warning: a clinic calling back, a delivery, a question about a bill. Call +81 90-6534-8689. We confirm the language and join as soon as we can.

If we are already on another call, we will get back to you as soon as we are free. When it is urgent, the phone is faster than the form.

When you can plan ahead

For a hospital visit, a meeting at city hall, a call with a landlord, a bank appointment, or a work meeting. Tell us the date and time (and the time zone, if you are outside Japan), the languages, and what the call is about. We confirm, and we are ready at the agreed time.

What we help with

Hospitals and clinics, city hall and immigration, landlords and real-estate agents, banks and phone carriers, business calls, and schools. If your situation is a little different, write or call. We will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Languages

We interpret Japanese ⇔ English and Japanese ⇔ Spanish, always both ways. We do not interpret English and Spanish when Japanese is not part of the call.

Video call interpreting

Some conversations are easier when you can see each other. Our professional interpreters join a video call and work both ways. The price is twice the phone rate: a ¥400 connection, ¥190 a minute, and a ¥1,500 minimum per call.

Trip scheduling

We help you plan a trip in Japan. That can include the itinerary, reservations, and how the days fit together. The rate is ¥5,000 an hour. Send a request with what you have in mind, and we will reply with a quote.

How it works

Four easy steps

Most calls have three people on the line: you, the other person, and our interpreter. We can also place the call for you. If you are not sure, we will help you set it up.

  1. Contact us

    Call, email, or use the form. A few words about the language, the time, and the situation are enough to start.

  2. We confirm the details

    We check the language, the time, and what the call is about, so everyone is ready.

  3. Talk with our interpreter

    You speak on the phone with our professional interpreter. We interpret both ways.

  4. Pay after the call

    Paying is easy. We email you a payment link and a receipt.
